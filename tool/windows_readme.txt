Vanguard Simulator for Windows
==============================

Keep the whole folder together. The .exe needs the files beside it: unzip
everything to somewhere like Documents, then run vanguard_simulator.exe.
Copying the .exe out on its own will not work.

The first time you run it, Windows will say it does not recognise the app --
"Windows protected your PC", from SmartScreen. That is because the file is not
signed by a paid-for certificate, not because anything is wrong with it. Click
"More info" and then "Run anyway".

Your decks are saved on this computer, in your own user folder. They are not
the same decks as the ones on your phone: to move a library across, open
Settings in the app, "Copy a backup", and paste it into Settings on the other
device. Nothing is ever sent anywhere -- there is no account and no server.

Two-player mode plays over your local network. The first time you host a game,
Windows will ask whether to allow it through the firewall; say yes for private
networks, or the other device will not be able to reach this one.
